## Overview

Choose from 17+ coaching personalities for your AI-generated training plan:

### Triathlon Experts
- **Coach Joe Friel** - Periodization pioneer, author of "The Triathlete's Training Bible"
- **Coach Matt Dixon** - Purple Patch founder, sub-9hr Ironman specialist
- **Coach Brett Sutton** - Olympic coach, high-volume training philosophy

### Cycling & Running Specialists
- **Coach Joe Friel (Cycling)** - Power-based training expert
- **Coach Jack Daniels** - VDOT running methodology creator
- **Coach Brad Hudson** - Elite marathon coach, adaptive training

### Science-Based Coaches
- **Dr. Stephen Seiler** - Polarized training pioneer
- **Dr. Andrew Coggan** - Power-based training scientist
- **Dr. Tim Noakes** - Exercise physiology researcher

...and 8 more styles! See full list: [TRAINER_STYLES_COMPLETE_GUIDE.md](../../TRAINER_STYLES_COMPLETE_GUIDE.md)

## How to Use

1. Navigate to AI Training Plan Generator
2. Click "Select Trainer Style" dropdown
3. Browse descriptions and select your preferred coach
4. Generate your personalized plan
