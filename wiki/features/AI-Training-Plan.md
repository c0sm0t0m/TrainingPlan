## Overview

The AI Training Plan Generator creates personalized training plans using ChatGPT, offering:

- **17+ Trainer Styles** - Choose a coaching personality that matches your preferences
- **100% Success Rate** - Auto-cleaning ensures valid JSON every time
- **Customizable Plans** - Sport, duration, volume, periodization, and more
- **Instant Generation** - Plans ready in seconds

## Quick Start

1. Click **"Create Training Plan"** button
2. Choose **"Generate with AI"** option  
3. Select your **trainer style** (e.g., "Coach Joe Friel", "Science-Based")
4. Fill in plan parameters (race date, weekly volume, etc.)
5. Click **"Generate Plan"**
6. Review and activate your plan
