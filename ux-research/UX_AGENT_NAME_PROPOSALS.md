# Product Name Proposals for TrainingPlan

**Generated**: January 2, 2026  
**Purpose**: Rebrand from generic "TrainingPlan" to memorable, distinctive name

---

## Top 3 Recommendations

### 1. **Tempo** ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐
**Tagline**: "Find your tempo"

**Why it works**:
- Universal concept: Every athlete seeks their optimal training tempo/rhythm
- Short, memorable, easy to pronounce globally
- Works across all sports (running cadence, cycling tempo, swim rhythm)
- Modern, sleek sound (similar to Stripe, Notion, Linear)
- Available domain: tempo.training, gettempo.com

**Brand positioning**: The modern training platform that helps you find your perfect training rhythm.

---

### 2. **Stride** ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐
**Tagline**: "Every stride counts"

**Why it works**:
- Athletic metaphor for progress and forward movement
- Warm, approachable, motivational
- Universal to all endurance sports
- Clear visual identity potential (forward arrow, footprint)
- Available domain: stride.training, usestride.com

**Brand positioning**: Progress-focused training platform that celebrates every step forward.

---

### 3. **Apex** ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐
**Tagline**: "Reach your apex"

**Why it works**:
- Powerful concept: Peak performance, summit achievement
- Premium feel (Apple-esque single word)
- Short, memorable, global pronunciation
- Strong visual identity (mountain peak, upward triangle)
- Available domain: apex.training, apextraining.co

**Brand positioning**: Elite training platform for athletes pursuing peak performance.

---

## Alternative Options

### 4. **Threshold** ⭐⭐⭐⭐
**Tagline**: "Push your threshold"
- Training term (lactate threshold, FTP)
- Suggests growth and breaking limits
- May feel technical for beginners

### 5. **Cadence** ⭐⭐⭐⭐
**Tagline**: "Your training rhythm"
- Specific cycling/running term
- Professional sound
- Longer word, less punchy

### 6. **Flow State** ⭐⭐⭐⭐
**Tagline**: "Train in the zone"
- Psychology term athletes know
- Unique positioning (mental + physical)
- Two words (less ideal for branding)

### 7. **Kinetic** ⭐⭐⭐⭐
**Tagline**: "Energy in motion"
- Scientific, modern
- Movement and energy association
- May feel cold/technical

### 8. **Ascent** ⭐⭐⭐⭐
**Tagline**: "Your training ascent"
- Clear progression metaphor
- Climbing/improvement association
- Available domains

### 9. **Breakaway** ⭐⭐⭐
**Tagline**: "Break away from the pack"
- Cycling term, competitive
- Longer word
- May feel niche to cyclists

### 10. **Uplift** ⭐⭐⭐
**Tagline**: "Elevate your training"
- Positive, motivational
- Less athletic/technical
- Generic feeling

## Decision Criteria

### Must Have
- ✅ Memorable (single word, < 8 letters)
- ✅ Global pronunciation (works in EN/DE/ES/FR)
- ✅ Available domain (.com or .training)
- ✅ Athletic connotation
- ✅ Not trademarked by major competitor

### Nice to Have
- ✅ Suggests progress/improvement
- ✅ Works across all sports
- ✅ Modern sound (not dated)
- ✅ Positive associations
- ✅ Unique in fitness space

---

## Recommendation

**Go with "Tempo"** for these reasons:

1. **Universal appeal**: Works for all athletes (runners, cyclists, triathletes)
2. **Modern sound**: Fits with current tech/SaaS branding trends
3. **Clear positioning**: Find your rhythm = training at optimal intensity
4. **Differentiation**: No major fitness app uses "Tempo" (Tempo Studio is different market)
5. **Brand flexibility**: Can expand beyond endurance sports later

### Domain Options
- `tempo.training` (preferred)
- `gettempo.com`
- `usetempo.com`
- `mytempo.app`
